# My Hotel Box - Complete Hotel Management Platform

## 30+ Modules | Mobile Responsive | 118 Test Iterations (100%)

### NEW: Demand Radar & Compset Intelligence (Phase 1 & 2)
1. **Demand Radar** — 90-day forward market intelligence: demand chart, WAP tracking, 7-day pickup change, pricing opportunity scatter map (underpriced/overpriced/peak/fair dots), lead time distribution, length of stay analysis, demand by lead time windows (URGENT/TACTICAL/STRATEGIC/HORIZON), day-of-week patterns. Day selectors: 30/60/90/180/365.
2. **Compset Intelligence** — Your hotel vs competitive set: occupancy/ADR/RevPAR ranking cards with rank badges (out of 12), sentinel insight, key insight diffs, ADR/Occupancy/RevPAR comparison charts, daily drill-down table, market context (segment + market hotels/rooms), tier distribution, neighbourhood breakdown. Day selectors: 7/14/30/60/90.

### Revenue Intelligence Suite (4 Features)
1. **Booking Pace & Pickup Velocity** — This year vs last year comparison, 24h pickup rate, velocity change alerts
2. **Revenue Forecast Engine** — AI-projected Revenue, ADR, Occupancy, RevPAR with YoY comparison
3. **Rate Recommendation Actions** — Daily AI to-do list with one-click Accept
4. **What-If Simulator** — Test rate changes before committing

### Revenue Sidebar (30+ modules)
