### Unified AI Price Suggestions
Combines ALL 4 data sources per month:
- **Historical** (2yr): avg/min/P25/max rates, seasonal patterns
- **Market Robot**: supply unavailability %, demand signal (+15% high / -5% low)
- **Event Intelligence**: mega/large event count, boost (+20%)
- **Competitor Prices**: avg competitor rate, positioning signal (+10% / -5%)
→ Outputs: AI Suggested Rate + Floor Price per month
→ "Apply All Floors to Strategy" feeds into Dynamic Pricing as 10th factor
